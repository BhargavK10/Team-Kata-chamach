2. Design the processing pipeline
I'd split your service into seven stages. Each stage should have one responsibility so you can test it separately and debug failures quickly.
1

Input validation and normalization
Check required fields, clean text, normalize timestamps and reject articles that lack enough information to process. Keep the original URL and source metadata.



2

Deduplication and company relevance
Identify repeated articles and determine whether the article actually concerns the target company. A company mentioned only in passing shouldn't automatically receive a risk event.



3

Risk classification
Use an LLM to decide whether the article contains a material corporate risk, its category, severity and status. Filter out routine news and irrelevant keyword matches.



4

Evidence and structured extraction
Extract the specific facts supporting the risk, a short evidence excerpt, event date, entities involved and a concise explanation. Never rely only on the article headline.



5

Deterministic risk scoring
Convert the extracted event into a numerical impact using severity, source quality, confidence, novelty and recency.



6

Historical comparison and aggregation
Compare the event with previous events, suppress repeated coverage of the same incident, update the company's score and calculate how much the score changed.



7

Response generation
Return the company score, risk level, newly detected events, key reasons, historical context and evidence links in a consistent JSON response for React.
